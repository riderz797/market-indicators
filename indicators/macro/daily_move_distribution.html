HOW TO READ IT

Gray bars — how often each size of daily move actually happened, across the asset's full history.

Dotted curve — what a normal distribution (the bell curve) predicts for the same mean and standard deviation. Where bars escape the curve in the tails, markets are wilder than the bell curve says they should be — and they always are.

Gold line — the latest daily move. The stats chip shows it three ways: standard deviations from the average (z), the percentile among all days on record (low = unusually negative day, high = unusually positive), and how often a move at least this large — in either direction — has historically occurred.

Charts show ±4.5 standard deviations. Days beyond that are counted in the stats and listed as extremes under each board.

DATA & METHOD
S&P 500Daily closes since Dec 1927 (Yahoo ^GSPC) — percent change
GOLDHistory: LBMA PM fix since Aug 1971, the end of the fixed gold window. Today's move: COMEX futures (GC=F), so the headline number is a close-to-close change matching a gold chart rather than the 3pm London fix-to-fix change — percent change
BRENT CRUDEHistory: Europe Brent spot since May 1987 (US Energy Information Administration, via FRED). Today's move: Brent futures (BZ=F), which are current where the spot print runs a few days behind — percent change
DXYUS Dollar Index since 1971 (ICE, via Yahoo) — percent change
BITCOINDaily closes since Sep 2014 (Yahoo BTC-USD) — percent change
US 10YR10-year Treasury yield since 1962 (CBOE ^TNX) — change in basis points
10YR − 2YR10-year minus 2-year Treasury spread since Jun 1976 (US Treasury constant maturities, via FRED) — change in basis points. Baked daily, not live: FRED publishes a day behind
MOVE INDEXICE Bank of America bond-market volatility since Nov 2002, stitched from Yahoo (deep history) and Barchart (current, and correct across bond-market holidays where Yahoo repeats the prior close) — percent change. Baked daily, not live

Moves are computed between consecutive available closes (gaps over 7 calendar days are skipped). Where a live feed exists, the latest move is fetched from Yahoo Finance each time you open this page; the historical distribution — and the latest move for the two baked-only boards — is built by fetch_daily_move.py, refreshed daily at 22:30 UTC by GitHub Actions.
