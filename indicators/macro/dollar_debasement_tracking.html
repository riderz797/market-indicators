HOW TO READ IT
0–35Dominance Intact — the dollar's reserve role is stable or strengthening.
35–65Erosion — reserve share declining, gold substitution active.
65–100Debasement — broad, sustained exit from dollar reserves.

The index blends the four forces below: reserve share 35%, Treasury flows 25%, dollar price 20%, gold rotation 20% — each scored 0–100, higher = more debasement. The green strip marks months where at least one dollar defense held: reserve share above 57%, official Treasury holdings rising over 3 years, or the dollar holding near a 10-year high. Today only the Treasury-flows defense is still standing — the reserve-share defense broke in 2025 for the first time on record, the dollar-high defense in 2023.
