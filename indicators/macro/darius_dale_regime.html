How it works. 42 Macro's K-shaped framework, split into two engines. The Real Economy marker (bottom of the K) is a fundamental nowcast of US growth (labor & income coincident data) versus inflation (Core PCE / Core CPI), each scored against the 2015–19 trend — 42 Macro's own reference baseline — blending level-vs-trend with the 6-month rate-of-change. The Financial Market marker (top of the K) is the Global Macro Risk Matrix: ~17 liquid markets each voting for the regime their momentum confirms; this is the regime KISS / Dr. Mo position on. A 3-week persistence filter holds this regime until a new one shows up for 3 straight weekly runs — a backtest of the last 4 years showed it cut whipsaw turnover ~2.6× and lifted the strategy's Sharpe. Recomputed weekly by a GitHub Action; the page falls back to the last snapshot if the data file is unavailable.
